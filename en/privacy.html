Our Commitment: RaccoonCorp respects your privacy. We collect only what is strictly required to provide and maintain our Android mobile applications, and we never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

1. Introduction & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, software tools, and related services published and operated by RaccoonCorp via Google Play and our official website.

By installing, accessing, or using our applications, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this policy. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please refrain from using our apps and uninstall them.

Data Controller:
RaccoonCorp
Contact: contact.raccooncorp@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

Depending on the specific application used, data collected may include:

  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Device model, Android OS version, anonymized IP address, app performance metrics, and crash stack traces to ensure app stability and debug issues.
  • Usage Statistics: Aggregated and anonymized interaction data (screens visited, features triggered) to optimize user experience.
  • Information You Provide Voluntarily: Your email address and correspondence content when you contact our support team.
  • Advertising Identifiers (if applicable): On ad-supported free applications, Google Play Advertising ID (GAID) may be used subject to your explicit consent settings.

No Sensitive Data: We do not collect biometric, health, payment card, religious, or political data.

3. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

We process collected data under the following legitimate legal bases:

  • To operate, maintain, and provide core application functionality (Contractual Necessity).
  • To debug crashes and ensure application security and reliability (Legitimate Interests).
  • To respond to user support inquiries and feedback (User Relationship).
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and tax obligations (Legal Compliance).

4. Android Device Permissions

Our applications may request specific system permissions on your Android device:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Needed to communicate with servers, fetch app content, or verify update availability.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to detect connectivity changes and manage offline capabilities gracefully.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: On Android 13 and above, enables opted-in reminders and alerts.

You can revoke any optional permission at any time directly through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > RaccoonCorp > Permissions).

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We may integrate trusted third-party SDKs to facilitate technical stability and distribution:

  • Google Play Services: Core system APIs, license verification, and secure distribution. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Anonymized performance tracking and crash diagnostics. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob (if applicable): Displaying contextual and personalized ads according to user consent. Google Ads Policy.

6. Data Security & Retention

We use industry-standard technical measures (such as TLS/HTTPS encrypted transmissions) to protect your data from unauthorized access or destruction.

Diagnostic logs and error metrics are retained for a maximum duration of 12 to 24 months before automatic deletion or irreversible anonymization. Support emails are archived only for the time necessary to resolve user requests and satisfy legal record obligations.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Our applications are not directed towards children under 13 (or under the minimum age defined by local jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors without parental consent. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under applicable data protection regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation and copies of personal data processed about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your personal data ("Right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction & Objection: Object to or restrict specific processing operations.
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in an accessible, structured digital format.

You also retain the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority.
